Job Title: Junior Data Analyst

Role Summary:
The Junior Data Analyst will support business teams by collecting, cleaning,
analyzing, and visualizing data to generate useful business insights.

Required Skills:
- Python
- SQL
- Power BI
- Basic Statistics
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization

Preferred Skills:
- Excel
- Tableau
- PostgreSQL
- Pandas
- NumPy

Experience Requirement:
- 0 to 2 years of relevant experience in data analysis, business intelligence,
  reporting, analytics, or a closely related field.

Education:
- Undergraduate degree or final-year study in Data Science, Computer Science,
  Information Technology, Engineering, Statistics, Business Analytics, or a
  related field.

Key Responsibilities:
- Clean and prepare datasets
- Write SQL queries
- Perform exploratory data analysis
- Create dashboards and reports
- Analyze trends and patterns
- Present findings to business stakeholders
